### AI Use Disclosure

I used artificial intelligence (AI) tools as a support resource during the development of this project. AI was mainly used to help identify and solve **syntax errors**, explain error messages, and suggest possible corrections when the code did not work as expected.

The AI was used as a debugging and learning aid, not to replace my own understanding or work. I reviewed the suggestions, tested the corrections, and made the final decisions about the code and implementation.
